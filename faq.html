Frequently asked questions

How does the recommendation engine work?

It analyses what you browse, buy, and save to surface products you're most likely to want — no personal data is ever sold.

Which payment methods are supported?

Checkout is powered by Paystack, supporting cards and bank transfers popular in Nigeria.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 2–5 business days depending on your location.

Can I return a product?

Yes — items can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused and in original packaging.

How do reward points work?

You earn points on every order and unlock Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers for better perks.
