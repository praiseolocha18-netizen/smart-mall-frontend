Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

We collect account details you provide (name, email) and activity data (browsing, orders) used to power recommendations and improve your experience.

To process orders, personalise recommendations, and communicate about your account. We never sell your personal data.

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and sessions are protected with JWT. Payments are handled by Paystack; we never store raw card details.
